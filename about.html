About Us

Our Story

"In Mexico, a barrio is more than just a neighbourhood. It's where people meet, eat, laugh, share stories and make memories. It's loud, lively, welcoming and full of character."

Taco Barrio was born from a simple love for authentic Mexican street food — bold flavours, fresh ingredients, and the kind of food that brings people together.

We're bringing that spirit to Queen Street in Auckland, New Zealand. Our little corner of Mexico in the heart of the city serves fresh Mexican tacos, burritos and street food with big flavour, excellent ingredients and a vibrant local atmosphere.

If you're searching for the best Mexican food in Auckland, tacos near Queen Street, or a casual Mexican restaurant in NZ, Taco Barrio is built for flavour, value and a memorable night out.
