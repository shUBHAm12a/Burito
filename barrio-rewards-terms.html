Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Barrio Rewards, Taco Barrio's loyalty programme.

  • Anyone can join Barrio Rewards by creating a customer account and joining the loyalty programme.
  • Barrio Rewards is currently available for eligible purchases made in-store through the Taco Barrio POS or kiosk.
  • Online orders and delivery orders do not currently earn or redeem Barrio Rewards points.
  • Points are earned on eligible food and drink items. The number of points earned may vary depending on the product.
  • Points will automatically be added to your Barrio Rewards account when you make an eligible purchase while logged in or identified as a Barrio Rewards member.
  • Once you have collected enough points, you can redeem them for available Barrio Rewards.
  • Rewards may be used together with eligible promotional or discount codes where supported.
  • Barrio Rewards points do not expire.
  • Points earned from cancelled or refunded orders may be removed from your account.
  • Points have no cash value.
